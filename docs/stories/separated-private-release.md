# Separated by One — private October 10 launch story

Scott approved a private TestFlight update that opens and autoplays the chart when Hub Ball launches after onboarding. This replaces the White Sox launch offer in this binary. Dismiss is a 44-point toolbar control; the sheet can also be swiped closed. Launch consumption belongs to AppModel and survives tab changes, root-view updates, dismissal and foreground returns. A new process presents it again. It remains accessible from Stories for replay/reopening.

Two cumulative-run lines build simultaneously for ten seconds across all thirteen 2026 regular-season meetings. Both start at zero and share x=0–13 and y=0–70. Build124 uses compiled wholeRunCounts presentation for counters, axes, endpoints, data rows and VoiceOver. Counters floor the interpolated values to whole runs; line geometry retains fractional precision and smooth motion. Other charts keep their original decimal formatting. Completed endpoints are directly labeled CWS 58 and CLE 57, with separated labels/leader lines so one run remains legible. Ending text explicitly distinguishes head-to-head wins CWS 7/CLE 6 from overall season wins CLE 85/CWS 84, then the dated October 10 Game 5 preview, 8 p.m. Eastern, series 2–2. “Tonight” refers to October 10, 2026; the payload is not a live schedule or scoreboard. Build125 holds the launch-only final payoff for six foreground seconds, or fifteen with accessibility text/VoiceOver, then dismisses automatically to the initial Postseason page. It scrolls the payoff into view. Replay resets the hold; background time and Sources/Data sheets pause it. Early Dismiss also lands on Postseason. Reduced Motion shows the completed chart immediately and uses the same readable hold. Manually opened library stories retain their ending until the reader navigates away and do not auto-redirect.

Facts are generated from official schedule/standings snapshots into separated-story-source.json and validated by build_separated_story.py. Spring training and four ALDS games are excluded from the graph. Through seventeen meetings including those ALDS games the totals would be CWS 73/CLE 78; these are not the chart totals. Original text and native graphics only; no third-party images, logos, video, music or article prose.

PrivateLaunchStory validates a separate bundled catalog/payload pair with identity and immutable SHA checks. It never enters StoryCatalogStore or its disk cache; production refresh/outage cannot remove it. Content uses existing chart-trajectory schema (minimum capability 2), without guessing or a custom comparison overlay. Short endpoint labels are a compiled private presentation option in this binary, not arbitrary downloaded UI or code. Existing charts keep their prior rendering when the option is absent; decoder capability remains3. All native resources are excluded from the web build.

Launch ordering is owned by AppModel.launchFlow/PostseasonLaunchFlow: Postseason first; eligible update notice before the story; Dismiss starts the story. The optional network/eligibility check has a two-second deadline that does not await uncooperative work; missing/disabled/failed/late targets are quiet. Successful update handoff waits for a foreground return; failure instructions must close before the story starts. Each process presents the launch story once.
